# Netso Energy AI Company

Netso Energy is an AI-native distributed-energy company. The founder remains the final authority for irreversible financial, contractual, legal, regulatory, ownership, and safety-critical decisions.

## Mission

Build and operate a high-velocity rooftop solar development and operating company where a small human team is amplified by a governed digital workforce.

## Operating model

Paperclip is the company control plane: people, agents, org chart, goals, tasks, routines, budgets, approvals, audit trails, and runtime coordination.

The existing AOS / agentic-harness repository is the execution and domain-intelligence layer: LangGraph workflows, capability-based tools, memory, evaluation, Netso venture context, financial validation, and cross-harness dispatch.

NEOS is the business source-of-truth layer that will progressively hold customer, site, project, PPA, financing, operational, knowledge, and decision records.

## Autonomy policy

- Observe: unrestricted internal analysis.
- Recommend: produce recommendations with evidence.
- Reversible execute: create/update internal work objects and drafts.
- Bounded execute: execute approved routine operations inside explicit limits.
- High-impact: prepare and route for founder approval.
- Human-only: money movement, legal execution, ownership changes, final safety-critical engineering approval, financing commitments, and irreversible governance changes.

## Founder

Board authority: Tazwar Mahtab.

The founder should receive decisions, exceptions, risks, and recommended actions rather than raw agent chatter.

## Executive team

- CEO / Maestro — strategy, prioritization, delegation, executive synthesis.
- COO — project delivery, procurement, installation, operational exceptions.
- CFO — project economics, financing, cash, portfolio capital allocation.
- CRO — commercial pipeline, prospect qualification, proposals, conversion.
- CTO / Energy Engineering — feasibility, generation, technical QA, engineering gates.
- Regulatory / Legal — PPA, contract, permitting, compliance, regulatory intelligence.

## Non-negotiable controls

1. Every material claim must be traceable to an authoritative source or explicitly labeled as an assumption.
2. No agent may use a shared master credential.
3. Side-effecting tools must be idempotent, permissioned, and auditable.
4. Final engineering and legal authority belongs to qualified humans.
5. Agent budgets must have hard stops.
6. The system must be able to pause a single agent or the whole workforce.
7. Agent changes, skills, policies, and company structure must be version-controlled.
