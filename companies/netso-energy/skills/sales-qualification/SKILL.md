# Sales Qualification

Score each prospect on:

- rooftop/site potential
- energy consumption and tariff economics
- counterparty credit quality
- decision-maker access
- strategic fit
- deployment probability
- urgency

Return the score, evidence for every major dimension, missing information, next action, and confidence. Never invent consumption, tariff, credit, ownership, or project information.
