# Founder Governance

Classify the requested action before acting.

## Human-only
Money movement, financing commitments, contract execution, ownership/governance changes, final safety-critical engineering approval, legally binding regulatory submissions.

## Approval-required
External commitments, customer pricing exceptions, material CAPEX, changes to canonical financial assumptions, new credentials, changes to autonomy level, or policy changes.

## Bounded execution
Internal task creation, research, analysis, draft documents, CRM enrichment, routine reporting, and reversible internal updates.

Always provide: proposed action, authority level, evidence, risk, reversibility, approver, and rollback path.
